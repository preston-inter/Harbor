# Harbor

A menu bar app for macOS 14 or later: a live Notch Island, Dock window previews, on-device dictation, clipboard history, window snapping and more.

## Download

Get **Harbor-x.y.z.dmg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/preston-inter/Harbor/releases/latest).

1. Open the DMG and drag **Harbor** into **Applications**.
2. Open Harbor. macOS says it can't check it for malicious software, because Harbor isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Harbor. You only need to do this once.

Harbor updates itself after that. To check by hand, choose **Check for Updates…** from its menu bar icon.

Everything runs on your Mac and nothing is uploaded.
